# Apple’s Notarization and Revocation

Apple’s existing "Kill Switches," pioneered through its notarization process, allow the company to remotely disable any application on any iPhone globally. This capability, combined with the US's $282 billion digital trade surplus, gives the US Executive branch a "Nuclear Option" in digital diplomacy. If a foreign political movement is deemed a threat to US interests, the infrastructure supporting that movement can be cryptographically "killed" at the OS level.

### Why This Matters for Apple Users

Apple's 'Nuclear Option' of remote revocation means your device is never truly yours. It is a leased environment where the landlord can evict your applications at any time. True ownership requires the ability to execute code without a third-party's cryptographic permission.
