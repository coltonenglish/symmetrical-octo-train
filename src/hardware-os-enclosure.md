# The Hardware/OS Enclosure: US Hegemony and the Single-HQ Risk

The most significant threat to digital sovereignty arises from the geographical concentration of the world's primary operating system vendors. Apple, Google, and Microsoft are all headquartered in the United States, creating what analysts term the "Single-HQ Risk." This centralization allows the US Executive branch to exert extraterritorial control over the political infrastructure of the entire world.

### Why This Matters for Industry & Global Sovereignty

The 'Single-HQ Risk' means that the world's digital infrastructure is vulnerable to the policy whims of a single nation's executive branch. For non-US nations, digital sovereignty is an illusion as long as the core OS and hardware attestation keys are held by US corporations. Diversification of hardware and the support for open-source, non-attested platforms are the only paths to true sovereignty.
