# The 2026 Android Developer Verification Mandate

In September 2026, Google will complete the rollout of its most restrictive developer mandate to date. All developers, including those distributing apps outside the Google Play Store (sideloading), must undergo rigorous identity verification.

| Verification Tier | Required Documentation | Identity Gating Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual Developer | Government-issued photo ID (Passport, Driver's License) + Address proof (Utility bill/Bank statement) | Permanent linkage of natural person to all distributed code. |
| Organization | D-U-N-S Number + IRS Documents (CP575, 147C) + SEC Filings | Commercial and legal taxonomization of software entities. |
| Verification Process | Email/Phone OTP + Google Search Console (Web verification) | Multi-factor attestation of developer legitimacy. |
| Distribution Fee | $25 registration fee for "Full Distribution" | Financial barrier to entry for independent or non-profit actors. |

Starting in late 2026, certified Android devices will use the Play Integrity API to remotely refuse the execution of binaries that are "unverified" or whose developer keys have been "revoked". This architecture provides Google with a global "Administrative Kill Switch." If a political movement relies on a specific mobile app for organization, Google can—at the behest of a government or its own policy teams—revoke the developer's identity, effectively uninstalling the tool from every "Certified" device in that region.

### Why This Matters for Developers

Identity gating for developers ends the era of anonymous software contribution. If every app must be linked to a government ID and a D-U-N-S number, the barrier to entry for independent or dissident software increases exponentially. Developers must advocate for decentralized distribution methods that bypass the Play Integrity API.
