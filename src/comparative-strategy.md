# Comparative Strategy: US vs. EU Models of Enclosure

The United States and the European Union have adopted distinct but complementary strategies for constructing the Digital Wall.

The US Strategy is one of Subversive Democracy via Identity-Gating. It focuses on the "originator" of the code. By making execution contingent on a verified legal identity, the US ensures that no "dark" movements can emerge anonymously. The power of the "Administrative Kill Switch" is used as a soft-power tool, where private companies (Apple, Google) enforce state priorities through "Terms of Service" violations, bypassing the First Amendment.

The EU Strategy is one of Regulatory Surveillance via Algorithmic Mandates. It focuses on the "content" of the communication. By mandating "safety by design" and the use of state-vetted detection tools, the EU creates a centralized surveillance architecture that forces providers to choose between compliance and exit. This strategy relies on the "Brussels Effect" to force these changes onto the global infrastructure.
