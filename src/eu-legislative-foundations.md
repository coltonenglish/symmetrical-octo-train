# Legislative Foundations of the Enclosure: The EU "Section 27" Framework

The structural integrity of the digital wall is anchored in a new class of "safety" legislation that mandates the integration of surveillance logic into the core of digital communication. The European Union’s Regulation on preventing and combating child sexual abuse (CSA), specifically provisions analogous to Section 27, serves as the primary legal catalyst for this transition.

### Why This Matters for the European Union

The mandate for client-side scanning represents a fundamental breach of the right to private correspondence. For EU citizens, this means that 'sovereignty' is being traded for a centralized surveillance apparatus managed by the EU Centre. Digital autonomy in Europe now depends on the ability of its citizens to demand transparent, non-intrusive security models that do not require state-vetted backdoors.
