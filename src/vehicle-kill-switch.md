# The Vehicle Kill Switch Precedent

The trend toward "Total Enclosure" is further evidenced by the January 22, 2026, Congressional vote (268-164) that mandated internet-connected "impairment detection" and "vehicle disabling" systems in all new cars sold in the US. This legislation requires cars to "passively monitor" driver behavior and "prevent or limit operation" if impairment is detected.

The implications for digital autonomy are profound. The same logic applied to vehicles—that a remote "kill switch" is necessary for "safety"—is being applied to general-purpose computing. Just as a car can be disabled without an officer's observation or probable cause, an application or a developer identity can be revoked without due process or a judicial warrant.
